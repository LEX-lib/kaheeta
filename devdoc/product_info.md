## Rationale

We will set what this app is all about. Kaheeta is a playful word from an Ilonggo word `kahita`, meaning wallet. This app was originally a mini app in Lexarium portfolio, but I have decided to make an app solely for this function. The inspiration of this is a typical Filipino wallet which contains IDs, membership cards, copy of vaccination, shopping list, receipts, Debit and Credit Cards, and so on. Kaheeta is the digital version of that wallet.
